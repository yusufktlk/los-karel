Brand Vision
LOS KAREL is envisioned as a premium contemporary clothing label where every
garment is treated as a collectible design object rather than a commodity.The digital
experience must communicate elegance, restraint and craftsmanship.The website
should feel closer to a luxury editorial magazine than a traditional marketplace.
    Typography, whitespace, photography and motion should reinforce the perception of

Brand Identity
Collections are inspired by cultural narratives including Anatolian heritage, Japanese
aesthetics, Nordic mythology, Brutalism, nature, mosaics and modern graphic art.The
front of garments remains minimal with a subtle logo while the back contains the
storytelling artwork.The website must mirror this philosophy through clean layouts and
immersive product pages.Collections are inspired by cultural narratives including
